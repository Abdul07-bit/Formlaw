Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

FormLaw reads your documents inside your own browser. This site does not upload them, store them, or send them to any server or AI service.

Your documents

  • Files you add and text you paste are processed in your browser tab only.
  • When you close or reload the page, they are gone. FormLaw does not save them in cookies or browser storage.
  • You are responsible for the confidentiality of documents on your own device.

What we collect

This site has no accounts, no forms that send data, and no analytics. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These logs are used to run and secure the site.

Cookies and third parties

FormLaw does not set cookies. It loads no analytics, advertising, font or script services from other companies. Its own files are served from the site's domain.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. If FormLaw later adds accounts, uploads or analytics, this policy will be updated before those features go live.
